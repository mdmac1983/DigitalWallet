1.0 - 2026-09-06 - Initial foundation: Wallet (Cards + IDs), Passwords, Contacts, and Finances (Income/Expenses) tabs with PIN-protected local encryption, plus an in-app browser with assisted transaction capture into Finances.
1.1 - 2026-09-06 - Renamed Finances to "Finances at a Glance". Replaced the in-app browser with statement import: upload a bank/credit card/investment statement (PDF or photo), on-device OCR + text extraction finds candidate transactions for you to review and confirm, and a consolidated monthly PDF (summary + full transaction detail) can be generated and shared. Original statement files are kept, encrypted, and viewable from the new Statements list.
1.2 - 2026-09-06 - Tuned statement parsing accuracy against real statement layouts: recognizes amounts where a minus sign is separated from the dollar figure by a space, picks the correct amount on lines with several dollar figures (multi-column activity tables), and understands statements that separate deposits/credits and withdrawals/debits into different tables instead of marking each amount's direction inline. Also skips "daily balance" tables so they're never mistaken for transactions.
1.3 - 2026-09-06 - New premium "navy + glass" look across the whole app. Added a Portfolio section to Finances at a Glance: manually enter your stock and crypto holdings (symbol, quantity, current price, and an optional cost basis) to see total value and gain/loss at a glance, alongside your income and expenses - it also now appears in the consolidated monthly PDF.
1.4 - 2026-09-06 - Fixed the bottom navigation and Wallet Cards/IDs tabs: the highlighted/selected tab was rendering in a dark navy that was nearly invisible against the new dark theme. It now uses the accent teal instead.
1.5 - 2026-09-06 - Added optional beginning/ending balance fields when importing a statement - type in what your statement itself says its balance was, and it shows on that statement in the Statements list and in the consolidated monthly PDF. This is a plain record of what the statement states, not a running balance the app calculates - a computed balance would silently go wrong the moment one transaction was missed or duplicated.
1.6 - 2026-09-06 - Added a 5th tab, Sales, for tracking services sold to clients: date, client ID, service, price, commission fee, partner fee, an always-accurate computed net fee, and a paid/unpaid status, with running totals up top. Added a branded splash screen on launch, and a faint logo watermark behind the Cards, IDs, Passwords, and Contacts lists.
1.7 - 2026-09-07 - Fixed card/ID/contact photos showing as flat gray boxes instead of the actual picture. Added a copy button next to the credit card number. Extended the logo watermark to Finances at a Glance, Sales, and both PIN screens. Rebuilt the monthly PDF's layout with real aligned columns and a logo watermark on every page. Finance entries can now use a custom label (Deposit, Withdrawal, Debit, Credit...) instead of Income/Expense. Added a Cash type to Portfolio holdings for uninvested brokerage cash. Added backup export/import under Settings (with a clear warning about matching PINs). Sales entries are now auto-numbered, and can be marked Partially Paid with an amount-paid and computed balance-due. Added a Buy Me a Coffee link in Settings.
1.8 - 2026-09-07 - Buy Me a Coffee link in Settings now points at the real page (buymeacoffee.com/orionmd) instead of the placeholder.
1.9 - 2026-09-07 - Cards: added a show/hide toggle for the CVV, billing address (street/city/state/zip) and credit limit fields. Card, ID, and contact photos are now cropped to fit their rounded frame instead of poking out past the corners, and tapping a photo enlarges it full-screen to view clearly - a small camera badge in the corner is now how you change the photo instead. Fixed Finances at a Glance: a Portfolio with several holdings could push the Import/Statements/Monthly PDF buttons and the add-entry button off screen with no way to reach them - Portfolio's holdings list can now be collapsed to a compact summary. Cards and IDs can now be put in whatever order you like - drag the handle on the right side of an entry to move it.
2.0 - 2026-09-07 - Passwords and Contacts can now be sorted Alphabetically, by Type, or Manually (drag to reorder) - tap the sort icon at the top of either list. Contacts gained a Category field (Family/Friend/Work/Business/Other) to make "by Type" sorting useful. Every list in the app - Cards, IDs, Passwords, Contacts, and Sales - now has a view-style option (tap the grid icon): Tiles (the original card look), Compact, or a flat List view, each remembered separately per list.
2.1 - 2026-09-07 - Builds are now signed with a real release key instead of the Android debug key - no functional changes, but this is required for installing over a previous version without uninstalling first, and for eventual distribution beyond direct sideloading.
